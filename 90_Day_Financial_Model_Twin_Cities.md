# 90-Day Financial Model — Twin Cities Restoration Leads
## Exclusive Water Damage / Mold / IAQ Leads to One Primary Contractor

**Assumptions (conservative starting point):**
- Average cost per lead (blended Google + Meta): $45–$85
- Close rate on exclusive leads: 25–40%
- Average job value: $2,500–$8,000+ (varies widely by water damage vs. full mold remediation)
- Ramp: start with modest ad spend and increase only after close rate is proven

---

### Monthly Ramp Example

| Month | Ad Spend | Leads | Est. Close Rate | Booked Jobs | Notes |
|-------|----------|-------|-----------------|-------------|-------|
| 1 | $2,000 | 25–40 | 20–25% | 5–10 | Learning phase, tight targeting |
| 2 | $3,500 | 40–70 | 25–35% | 10–25 | Optimize keywords & creative |
| 3 | $5,000+ | 60–100+ | 30–40% | 18–40 | Scale winners |

**Cost per booked job target:** Aim to keep it well below average job margin. Track actual close rate and average ticket in the dashboard.

---

### Sensitivity
- Higher urgency (true emergency water) often closes faster and at higher ticket sizes.
- Mold inspection/testing can be lower ticket but frequently leads to remediation work.
- Partnership referrals typically have lower acquisition cost and higher trust/close rate.

---

### Tracking Required
- Cost per lead by channel
- Lead-to-appointment rate
- Appointment-to-proposal rate
- Proposal-to-close rate
- Average job value by service type

Update the model after the first 30 days of real data from the dashboard.
