**How it works:**
1. Homeowners submit a short qualifying form (service type, urgency, property, ZIP, contact).
2. Leads are scored for urgency, decision-maker status, and location.
3. Qualified leads appear in your dashboard with full contact details.
4. You contact them, manage the job, and bring in specialists as needed.
5. Status updates (New → Contacted → Appointment → Proposal → Won/Lost) keep the pipeline visible.
