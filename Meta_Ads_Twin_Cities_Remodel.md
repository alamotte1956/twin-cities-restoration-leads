# Meta (Facebook / Instagram) Ads — Twin Cities Restoration
## Water Damage, Mold & Indoor Air Quality Leads

**Objective:** Leads (Instant Form or Website Form)  
**Goal:** Exclusive restoration inquiries from homeowners in priority Twin Cities ZIPs.

---

### Targeting
- Priority ZIPs or tight metro radius matching the contractor’s service area
- Age 30–65+
- Homeownership / household income layers where available
- Interests (secondary): home improvement, insurance, real estate, HGTV

Test cold geo + age, lookalikes of past customers, and retargeting of website visitors / video viewers.

---

### Creative Concepts
1. **Emergency before/after or process video** — water extraction, drying equipment, mold removal. Vertical, 9–20 seconds.
2. **“What to do after water damage”** short educational + CTA.
3. **Mold inspection / air quality** static or carousel explaining the process.
4. **Testimonial** from a local homeowner who had water or mold handled quickly.

**Primary text example:**  
Water damage or mold in your Twin Cities home? Get fast help from one licensed local restoration company. We handle extraction, dry-out, mold remediation, inspection, and air quality testing — and you won’t be shared with other contractors.

**Headline:** Emergency Water & Mold Help  
**CTA:** Get Help Now

---

### Form Questions
Service needed, urgency, property type, ZIP, name, phone, email, decision-maker status, brief description.

Add a filter if desired (e.g., “Is there active water or visible mold?”).

---

### Notes
Emergency creative usually outperforms lifestyle creative for these services. Feed Meta quality signals (leads the contractor marks as contacted or won) so the algorithm improves.
