# Google Ads Campaign Structure — Twin Cities Restoration Leads
## Water Damage, Mold & Indoor Air Quality — Exclusive to One Contractor

**Goal:** Capture high-intent searches for water damage, dry-out, mold, and air quality services and send them to the qualifying landing page.

---

### Recommended Campaign Structure

| Campaign | Focus | Budget Share |
|----------|-------|--------------|
| Water Damage / Dry Out | Emergency water & dry-out keywords | 40% |
| Mold Remediation & Mitigation | Mold removal and mitigation | 30% |
| Mold Inspection & IAQ Testing | Testing and inspection | 20% |
| Brand / Competitor (optional later) | | 10% |

Use Search + Local Services Ads where eligible. Separate campaigns so budget follows the services that close best.

**Match types:** Phrase and Exact preferred. Strong negative list.

**Locations:** Twin Cities metro, priority ZIPs first.

---

### High-Intent Keyword Examples

**Water Damage / Dry Out**
- "water damage restoration Minneapolis"
- "water damage cleanup St Paul"
- "dry out services Twin Cities"
- "emergency water extraction Minneapolis"
- "flooded basement cleanup Minneapolis"
- "water damage contractor near me"

**Mold**
- "mold remediation Minneapolis"
- "mold removal St Paul"
- "mold mitigation Twin Cities"
- "mold remediation contractor Minneapolis"
- "black mold removal Minneapolis"

**Inspection & Testing**
- "mold inspection Minneapolis"
- "mold testing St Paul"
- "indoor air quality testing Minneapolis"
- "air quality test Twin Cities"
- "mold inspection near me"

**Negatives (account-wide):** DIY, how to, free, cheap, jobs, hiring, careers, commercial (if residential only), renters insurance only, etc.

---

### Ad Copy Direction
Headlines emphasizing speed and exclusivity:  
“Emergency Water Damage – Twin Cities”  
“Licensed Mold Remediation – Free Assessment”  
“Indoor Air Quality Testing – Fast Results”

Description: One local restoration company manages the full job. Exclusive service — no shared leads.

---

### Tracking
Primary conversion = form submission (service type, urgency, ZIP). Review search terms weekly. Shift budget toward services with the best close rate.
