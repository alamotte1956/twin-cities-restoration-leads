# Twin Cities Restoration Lead Program — Strategy
## Exclusive Water Damage, Mold & Indoor Air Quality Leads to One Primary Contractor

**Model:** Exclusive leads only. One primary restoration company receives every qualified lead and manages the full job (subcontracting specialists as needed).

**Market:** Minneapolis–St. Paul metro (Twin Cities), Minnesota.

**Services covered:**
- Water damage restoration
- Dry out services
- Mold mitigation
- Mold inspection
- Mold remediation
- Indoor air quality testing

---

### Why This Model Wins
- Higher close rates (exclusive leads typically convert 20–40%+ vs. 5–15% on shared platforms).
- Lower cost per booked job once volume and close rates are proven.
- Trust: homeowners know only one company is contacting them.
- Control: the primary contractor owns the relationship and can sub out drying, testing, or remediation specialists.

---

### Lead Quality Filters & Scoring
Scoring prioritizes:
- Urgency (emergency water damage or ASAP mold issues score highest)
- Decision-maker / property owner status
- Priority Twin Cities ZIPs
- Sufficient detail in the request
- High-value service types (active water damage, full remediation, dry-out)

Leads that fail minimum thresholds can be filtered or credited before the contractor sees them.

---

### Primary Acquisition Channels
1. **Google Search + Local Services Ads** — Highest intent for “water damage restoration Minneapolis,” “mold remediation St Paul,” etc.
2. **Meta (Facebook/Instagram)** — Before/after and emergency-response creative targeting priority ZIPs.
3. **Partnerships** — Insurance agents, property managers, realtors, plumbers, and HVAC companies.
4. **SEO / Content** — Local emergency guides and cost/process pages that compound over time.
5. **Retargeting** — Website visitors who didn’t submit.

---

### Economics Snapshot
Exclusive restoration leads typically cost more per lead than shared marketplace leads but deliver far better close rates and job values. Track cost per booked job, not just cost per lead. Emergency water and mold jobs often justify higher acquisition costs because of urgency and ticket size.
